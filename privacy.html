Mirobola Privacy Policy

November 5, 2026

Who we are

Mirobola publishes short videos on its own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. Mirobola's publishing tool is used only by its owner, for the owner's own accounts.

What the tool does

It uploads videos and their text to the Mirobola accounts. Later, it will read the statistics and public comments of these accounts, to choose upcoming topics.

Data

  • The tool does not collect any data about viewers, and does not sell or share any.
  • The access tokens for the Mirobola accounts are kept on a private server, readable only by the owner, and are used solely to publish and to read the statistics of these accounts.
  • The comments that are read are used only to spot topic ideas, and are kept only as reworded topics.

Third-party services

  • The tool uses YouTube API Services: by interacting with Mirobola's videos on YouTube, you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • It also uses the Meta (Instagram) and TikTok APIs, each under its own rules.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • The owner can revoke the tool's access to the accounts at any time: from https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions for Google, and from the app settings of Meta and TikTok.
  • Any request to delete data should be sent to the contact address below and will receive a reply within 30 days.
